Parking in Sofia

Blue Zone €1.02 / hour

Central Sofia · Max. 2 hours · SMS 1302

Green Zone €0.51 / hour

Around the centre · Max. 4 hours · SMS 1303

💳 For tourists: Pay with a bank card through the Sofia Plus or Urbo app. SMS payment may not work with foreign SIM cards. Parking meters are also available.

⚠️ Always check the signs and make sure your payment is confirmed.

Useful Local Tips

Currency: The currency is Euro (EUR).
Tap water: Safe to drink.
Communication: Bulgarians traditionally shake their head for "yes" and nod for "no".
Public toilets: Some may require a small fee, so carrying a few coins can be useful.
Safety: Sofia is generally safe, but keep an eye on your belongings in crowded areas and on public transport.

Bulgarian Food to Try

Shopska Salad
Tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, onions and Bulgarian white cheese.
Bulgarian Yogurt
Thick and creamy traditional yogurt, often enjoyed with breakfast, honey or fruit.
Banitsa
A popular filo pastry made with eggs and cheese.
Kebapche
Grilled minced meat rolls, usually served with fries, salad or lyutenitsa.
Kavarma
Slow-cooked meat and vegetables traditionally served warm in a clay pot.
Tarator
A refreshing cold soup made with yogurt, cucumber, garlic and dill.
Rakia
A traditional Bulgarian fruit spirit often enjoyed as an aperitif.
Bulgarian Wine
Try local varieties such as Mavrud, Melnik or Rubin.
